# Deciphering Earth's History: the Practice of Stratigraphy

![](images/dehps.png){: width="25%" style="float:right; margin-left:5em;" }

The chapter provided below is part of a Geoscience in Practice volume, published by the Geological Society, London on how to apply the wide range of different stratigraphical techniques available and entitled: '_Deciphering Earth's History: the Practice of Stratigraphy_' (2022).
 
The whole volume is available in print and eBook formats via the Geological Society website:

* **Print**: <https://www.geolsoc.org.uk/bookshop/products/deciphering-earths-history-the-practice-of-stratigraphy/>
* **eBook**: <https://www.lyellcollection.org/doi/book/10.1144/GIP1>
